What is Pinnacle Pipeline?

All over the world young-adult athletes are forced to make tough sacrifices when deciding whether to pursue their dream or focus on their future career. High-level sports get in the way of gathering professional experience and, additionally, many sports do not have the financial capabilities of ice-hockey or football – forcing many promising young athletes to give up on their dream. Pinnacle Pipeline aims to eliminate this group by connecting athletes to businesses.
